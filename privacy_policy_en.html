General Information

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile application FarsiCourse ("the App"). The App is actually provided as a free service and is designed to protect your privacy.

No Collection of Personal Information

  • No Data Gathering: We do not collect, store, log, or share any personal data.
  • Audio Processing: Any voice or audio processing happens entirely offline on your local device.
  • No Server Storage: No audio files or voice data are ever transmitted to our servers.

Information Collected by Third Parties

1. Platform Providers (Apple App Store)

The App is distributed via the Apple App Store. Apple may independently collect technical metadata under its own privacy policy. This includes download statistics, device models, or crash reports. We only receive aggregated, anonymous statistics from Apple and do not have access to any personal identities through these platform-provided statistics.

2. Infrastructure & Hosting (Cloudflare R2)

To provide you with the App's media assets (audio files), we use Cloudflare R2 Object Storage, operated by Cloudflare, Inc. (101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). For users within the European Union, European inquiries may also be addressed to Cloudflare Germany GmbH (Rosental 7, 80331 Munich, Germany).

When your device downloads these assets, Cloudflare automatically processes technical data necessary for data transfer, network routing, and infrastructure security:

  • Technical Identifiers: General device metadata, operating system version (visionOS), and App version.
  • Network Data: Your IP address (processed temporarily to fulfill the download request and Route network traffic efficiently).

This data is processed strictly to deliver the required content to your device and to ensure the operational security and stability of our data storage. We do not use this data to track you or create personal profiles. Cloudflare, Inc. participates in and has certified its compliance with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For more information, please review the official Cloudflare.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not directly collect, sell, rent, or share any personal information of our users with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

As disclosed in the section above, technical data (such as IP addresses and device identifiers) is processed strictly by our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) solely to fulfill automated download requests and ensure app stability. This processing does not constitute a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law.

  • Do Not Track: We do not track your behavior across third-party websites or online services.
  • Your Rights: Consumers residing in California have the right to request information regarding how their data is processed, as outlined in this policy.

Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13, as no data is collected from any user.
